# Governance

Partial Association Explorer is maintained by its authors. Maintainers review
issues and pull requests, decide release scope, and are responsible for the
scientific and software quality of accepted changes.

Contributions are evaluated for statistical correctness, clarity, test
coverage, compatibility with the documented workflow, and maintainability.
Substantive changes to association measures or inference procedures should
include references, tests against known results or independent software, and an
explanation of the design trade-offs.

Decisions are normally made by consensus among the maintainers. When consensus
cannot be reached, the corresponding author coordinates a documented decision
after consulting the other maintainers. Contributors retain credit through the
Git history and release notes; significant scholarly contributions may warrant
authorship subject to the agreement of all authors and the target venue's
authorship policy.
